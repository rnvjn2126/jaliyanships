PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026. This draft requires approval by Jaliyan Sustainable Ship Recycling Pvt. Ltd. and, where appropriate, legal counsel before publication as a final policy.

Information collected

If you submit an enquiry, the form requests your name, email address, optional subject and message. Do not submit confidential, sensitive or unnecessary personal information through the form.

Purpose and processing

The enquiry information is intended to receive and respond to your request. The website does not currently use analytics, advertising pixels or account registration. Static site hosting may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver the site.

Third-party form provider

Enquiries are transmitted directly from your browser to FormSubmit, an external form-processing provider. FormSubmit processes the enquiry under its own terms and privacy practices. The company must approve the selected recipient, retention period, access controls and any processor agreement before collecting production enquiries.

Cookies and browser storage

The website does not intentionally set first-party analytics or marketing cookies, and it does not use local storage, session storage, IndexedDB or service workers. Hosting and the form provider may process information in connection with requests. The live deployment should be rechecked after every provider or hosting change.

Retention, sharing and security

Retention periods, authorised recipients, deletion processes and security measures have not yet been approved. The company should document them before production use. Information should only be shared where needed to handle an enquiry or meet a legal obligation.

Your requests

A verified privacy contact and request process must be approved and added before publication. Until then, use the contact form and state that the request concerns privacy.

Legal readiness

This draft is designed to support clear notice about the collection and purpose of enquiry data. It is not legal advice and does not represent a claim of legal compliance. The company should review applicable obligations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and notified Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, before approving this policy.
