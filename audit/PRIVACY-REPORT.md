# Privacy Report

The contact form collects name, email address, optional subject and enquiry message and sends it to FormSubmit. Privacy and terms drafts were added to the site and labelled for company/professional approval.

Missing approvals include the approved privacy contact, retention schedule, authorised recipients, processor review, deletion process, lawful processing basis, governing law and dispute terms. The [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) and [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025) require company/legal review before finalisation.
