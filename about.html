ABOUT JALIYAN SHIPS

Turning end-of-life vessels into reusable resources.

Jaliyan Ships operates in the ship breaking and ship recycling sector. The company’s work centres on dismantling end-of-life vessels and recovering materials that can be returned to productive use.

Our aim is to approach each project with care, coordination and attention to responsible working practices. This website will be updated with verified company history, facilities, operational capacity and project information.
